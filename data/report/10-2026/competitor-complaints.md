# Competitor complaints: Zomato vs Swiggy vs Blinkit, October 2026

Scope: October 2026 (01-10-2026 to 31-10-2026), Google Play India, English reviews, 10 keyword themes applied to written 1-2 star reviews.

## Headline numbers

| App | Reviews | Average rating | 1-star share | Written negative reviews (1-2 stars, over 20 chars) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zomato | 19,593 | 3.98 | 18.7% | 2,956 |
| Swiggy | 12,342 | 3.65 | 27.5% | 2,839 |
| Blinkit | 41,522 | 4.16 | 14.3% | 4,979 |

## Top 3 complaints per app

Ranked by raw keyword count. Quotes are masked.

### Zomato
1. **support** (26.9% of written negative reviews): Customer care is a deflection loop. Bots and canned chat options, redirects to email with a 24-hour wait, no phone line, and no resolution for missing, wrong or stale food. Users say Zomato sides with the restaurant even with photo proof.
   > "your customer support sucks which food delivery app thinks that they will connect the user in the next 24 hours"
2. **late** (16.5%): Orders arrive 1 to 2+ hours after a 20 to 30 minute estimate, riders are batched with several orders, and the ETA keeps changing.
   > "take more then an hour to deliver everytime. also charges additional amount for nothing."
3. **price** (14.9%): Menu prices above the restaurant's own or offline prices, plus stacked platform, packaging and delivery fees.
   > "Delivery and platform fees are too high. Further, higher cost of food as compared to offline prices for same food."

### Swiggy
1. **support** (29.2%): Chatbot-only support. Users cannot reach a human, get canned replies, or wait days for email responses on delayed, wrong or undelivered orders and refunds. Instamart issues stay open after repeated tickets.
   > "poor customer service...ai chatbot is not helping. not able to connect with an agent directly..asking for a photo as proof for items that are not delivered and cold food is absurd"
2. **late** (21.8%): Orders arrive 1 to 2+ hours after a 25 to 35 minute ETA, the ETA stays fixed or misleads, the delivery partner is reassigned mid-order, and many orders end in a late cancellation.
   > "I had the worst delivery experience with this useless app it shows 30 min but takes more then 1hrs and even offers are useless ."
3. **cancel** (16.3%): Swiggy or the restaurant auto-cancels after a 30 to 120 minute wait with no compensation, and a user cancel within 1 to 2 minutes is charged up to 100% of the order value.
   > "Worst service by Swiggy. After waiting for the promised time, they simply canceled my order after 45 minutes. It's the worst service by Swiggy."

### Blinkit
1. **support** (15.9%): Support that does not resolve. Scripted chat replies, responses slower than 20 minutes, returns or replacements refused as "beyond policy", no call helpline.
   > "horrible customer service if I ask any help to return the item always tell it's beyond our policy"
2. **price** (14.4%): Handling, delivery, small-cart, surge and rain fees stacked even on larger baskets.
   > "Why do you charge so many extra fees? Even when the amount is over ₹400, you are still adding a handling fee. What is the point of this"
3. **late** (9.8%): The 10-minute promise breaks. Orders take 20 to 60 minutes, the ETA resets, and delays are worst in smaller cities.
   > "worst service shows 15 min delivery but it takes more than 1 hour for delivery"

## Takeaways

- **Zomato is better than or level with Swiggy on every theme per 1,000 reviews.** Support is 40.6 vs 67.1, late delivery 25.0 vs 50.1, cancellations 12.6 vs 37.6. Its average rating is 3.98 vs 3.65 and 1-star share is 18.7% vs 27.5%.
- **Where Zomato loses ground is the mix, not the volume.** Food quality is 8.8% of Zomato's written negatives vs 6.2% at Swiggy. Payment failures are 5.2% vs 4.3%. Wrong or missing items are 9.2% vs 8.2%. These are also the themes where Zomato's advantage is smallest per 1,000 reviews: quality 13.3 vs 14.2 (about the same), payment 7.8 vs 9.9, missing 13.9 vs 18.9.
- **Support is still Zomato's biggest single complaint, and it is the same story as Swiggy.** 796 written negatives (26.9%), 40.6 per 1,000. Both apps are described as bot-first with no human route, so fixing it is a lead, not a catch-up.
- **Price is about as bad as Swiggy in share (14.9% vs 15.3%).** Users on both apps blame stacked platform and delivery fees, and some Zomato reviewers say prices are lower on Swiggy.
- **Blinkit is context only.** It is a grocery app (also owned by Eternal), so its 10-minute promise and handling fees are not Zomato's comparison. Zomato is higher than Blinkit on support (40.6 vs 19.0), late (25.0 vs 11.8) and cancellations (12.6 vs 6.1), but those gaps partly reflect a different product. Themes that do not apply cleanly to Blinkit: food quality (its quality problem is freshness and expiry, which the keywords miss), wrong or missing items (grocery wording is not matched), delivery-partner behaviour in a restaurant sense, and restaurant-linked cancellations.

## Caveats

- Themes are keyword regexes, not AI labels. A review can match several themes, so counts do not add to the written negative total. Shares are of written negative reviews only.
- English only. Hinglish in Latin script (for example "ghatiya", "nahi") is missed, so all counts are understated.
- Reviews were read with an unofficial scraper (`google-play-scraper`) from the public Play Store page. Ratings are the review-level average for the month, not the Play Store's weighted score.
- Only about a quarter (Zomato 25%), a third (Swiggy 32%) and 35% (Blinkit) of reviews have text, so theme numbers rest on a minority of reviews. Per-1,000 rates also depend on how many reviews each app's users write.
- The `late` regex is loose (it matches "hour" and "N min"), `support` matches "chat" and "agent", and `price` matches "charges". These inflate those themes on all three apps, and overlap between `late`, `support` and `cancel` is high.
- Swiggy reviews also cover Instamart and Dineout, so some complaints are about grocery or table booking, not food delivery.
- Blinkit has no grocery vocabulary (expired, damaged, leaking, wrong product), so about 600 written negatives are uncounted and `missing` and `quality` are badly understated for it.
- Rules and numbers recomputed on 15-11-2026 from the CSVs with `load()` and `COMPLAINTS` from `scripts/build_report_data.py` (uncommitted version on disk). All 10 themes per app matched the supplied figures, so none needed fixing.
