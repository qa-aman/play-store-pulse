    How these numbers are made
    • Source: public Google Play reviews of the Zomato Android app, read with the open-source google-play-scraper library. English reviews from India only, so Hindi and other languages are not included.
    • Times are in IST. The period runs from 01-10-2026 00:00 to 31-10-2026 23:59.
    • A written review has more than 20 characters of text. Shorter ones count as star-only.
    • Themes are keyword matches, a first cut. AI labelling of every written review would make them sharper.
    • Quotes are picked by the script and masked for phone numbers, emails and long ids.
